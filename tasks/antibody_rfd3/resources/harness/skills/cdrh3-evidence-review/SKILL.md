---
name: cdrh3-evidence-review
description: Review candidate provenance, measured evidence and uncertainty without generating or inventing measurements.
---

Read this generation's peer catalog and query experiment_history for observations and pending designs. Check each proposed ID, its sequence, constraints, role of origin, backbone and generation settings. The supplied candidate catalog is a snapshot of actual peer submissions, not an energy table. Exclude measured or pending sequences and duplicate H3s. Choose only IDs present in the catalog.

In submit_designs put a concise factual account in evidence, distinguishing actual measured energies and their sequences from constraints, provenance and unmeasured candidate properties. Put proposed mechanisms or explanations in hypotheses; write "none" when there are none. Note when few data prevent a strong ranking. Do not describe a predicted contact, affinity gain, structural mechanism, generated geometry, or ProteinMPNN output as a measured result. Absolut scores sequence in its lattice setting and does not consume the generated coordinates. Mock energies do not demonstrate biological activity.

The reviewer has no generation budget. Review the complete catalog and finish with submit_designs using one or more distinct catalog IDs and both required text fields; selecting every ID is not required. The generating roles' proposals form the candidate pool, while this review records evidence and hypotheses.
